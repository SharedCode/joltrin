How that shapes Joltrin

  • Stop the risky step, then say what comes first.

    A release pipeline does not just fail a bad deploy. It tells you which check to run. Joltrin works the same way: it blocks a risky agent step, like dropping a database, until the steps before it are recorded, and tells the agent what to do first.

  • Let people try it before they trust it.

    You can run the barrier in your browser, and the core is open source under the MIT license, so you can read exactly what it does.

  • Keep the workflow readable.

    The automation I have seen last is the kind a new engineer can follow in an afternoon. I try to keep Joltrin's rules and checks that plain.
